NLP & Machine Learning II

This week splits into two powerful tracks. Days 1–2 introduce Natural Language Processing — how machines turn messy human text into numbers they can learn from (tokenization, stemming, POS, NER, Bag-of-Words). By Friday you can build a text classifier end-to-end and choose the right model for structured data.

Week 6 Cheat Sheet

NLP Pre-processing Checklist

1. Lowercase
2. Remove URLs / HTML / special chars (task-dependent)
3. Tokenize (word_tokenize or spaCy)
4. Remove stop-words (optional)
5. Stem or Lemmatize
6. Vectorize → CountVectorizer or TfidfVectorizer
7. Train classifier (LogisticRegression, LinearSVC, DecisionTree…)

Key Metrics

Precision = TP / (TP + FP)
Recall    = TP / (TP + FN)
F1        = 2 * P * R / (P + R)
